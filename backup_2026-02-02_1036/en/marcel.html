About Me

Impossible doesn't exist! That's my motto.

At the age of three, I lost my eyesight. Nevertheless, I wanted to experience everything my friends and siblings could do:

  • I stood on roller skates playing hockey
  • I went to ice hockey games and concerts alone
  • I flew alone to language stays

With the help of kind people, a positive mindset, a functioning and engaged brain, courage and a bit of luck, anything is possible.

Meanwhile, I lead Swisscom's internal film and photo team. Film team? Yes. My employer Swisscom gave me the opportunity to live out my strengths. As a storyteller, strategic manager for the topic and apprentice supervisor, I can help shape communication and the future.

In 2019, I completed my Master's degree in Brand and Marketing Communication with a focus on Storytelling. At the same time, I founded the company help2type. I wanted to apply theory in practice. With help2type, I solved my own problem and developed a business model from it.

After being asked several times for keynotes, I attended coaching with a director and Top100 Speaker in Europe. That's how I also immersed myself in this world 6 years ago and now share my experiences and insights with great commitment.

My professional journey can be found at www.linkedin.com/in/marcelroesch
